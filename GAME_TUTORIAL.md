# Shooter Sam — Game Tutorial

## Objective

Shooter Sam is a third-person sci-fi shooter where you must eliminate all enemy soldiers in the level.

The game features **one playable level**. Once all enemies are eliminated, the level is completed and can be played again.

## Controls

### Player Movement

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **W** | Move forward |
| **S** | Move backward |
| **A** | Move left |
| **D** | Move right |
| **Space** | Jump |

### Aiming

- **Mouse** — Aim and control the player's orientation.
- Move the mouse left/right to rotate the player and aim horizontally.
- Move the mouse up/down to raise or lower the weapon's aim.
- The on-screen crosshair indicates the current aiming point.

### Shooting

- **Left Mouse Button** — Fire the weapon.

## Gameplay

1. Explore the sci-fi environment and locate the enemy soldiers.
2. Aim using the mouse and fire with the **Left Mouse Button**.
3. Enemy soldiers will detect and pursue the player.
4. Enemies can also attack the player with their weapons.
5. The player's **health bar** is displayed on the screen.
6. Eliminate all enemy soldiers to complete the level.
7. Defeated enemies remain on the ground.
8. If the player is defeated, a death animation plays and the level restarts.
9. After eliminating all enemies, the level can be played again.
